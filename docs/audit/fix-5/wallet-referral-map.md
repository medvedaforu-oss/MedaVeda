## Dates, amounts and immutability

- `available_at`/`expires_at`: stamped at settlement from live hold and expiry settings; Razorpay legacy 20261104100000:399 and Play 20261019151000:84. Duration is not separately captured. Pending release does not edit these stamps.
- `status_changed_at`: append-only trigger stamps pending→available/cancelled with now(); amount/source/created_at/available_at/expires_at immutable. FK anonymization may clear user/referral/order/reverses references. DELETE/TRUNCATE forbidden.
- Ledger CHECKs: credit/debit/reversal types; pending/available/cancelled statuses; amount>0 numeric(10,2); closed sources; pending date required; only credits pending. Unique reward/referral, debit/order, restore/order, reversal/reverses_id.
- Ledger RLS own/admin SELECT only; client writes blocked by privileges, policies and block_client_writes. Internal helpers service only. Admin actions call am_i_admin (including current MFA gate).
- Reservations: positive amount, order FK cascade; live iff unexpired created order. First-discount ownership and purchase identity hashes survive replay/deletion policy. Orders retain immutable price/entitlement snapshots and positive payable checks.
- Expiry setting: integer nullable, CHECK null OR >0; zero currently rejected. Admin blank already writes null. Settings audit captures changes; it does not apply them retroactively to ledger.
