### Authentication, recovery and deletion

- **Registration/login:** validated email/mobile/password, profile trigger/phone uniqueness; email sign-in goes to Supabase Auth, mobile sign-in through `login-with-mobile`. ### Offline grace and identity retention

Seven-day grace is **604,800,000 ms since successful online note authorization**, not download age or last open. Missing/legacy/corrupt/future timestamps fail closed; time moving backward invalidates verification. Online 401/403 denies and removes the download; transport/server/429 failures can use a recent verification. Viewer checks on open, foreground/auth changes and every 60 seconds. Expiry in an already open foreground viewer can therefore be observed at the next timer check, plus request timeout; it is not an exact real-time DRM cutoff. Wall-clock and writable local metadata are not tamper-proof offline licensing. Fresh tests cover day eight, rollback persistence, 401/403, legacy downloads and account cleanup. B03 remains.

Identity cutoff is **180 days from `first_purchase_at`**, including active accounts; it is not a new 180 days starting at deletion. The purge deletes email hash, phone hash and original account ID together and has a cutoff index/service-only EXECUTE. Scheduled execution is deliberately commented. Once removed, an identity may again qualify for first-purchase benefits subject to other purchase/referral checks; this follows the chosen finite retention policy, not an accidental reason to retain indefinitely. Published wording promises deletion, so scheduling and monitoring are release requirements. See B04.
