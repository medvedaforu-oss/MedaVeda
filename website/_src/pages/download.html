Get the MedVeda app

MedVeda is a BAMS study app for Android. Video lectures and MCQ tests are free; PDF notes are premium.

Android

Use the official Google Play link for MedVeda (Android package in.medveda.app).

{{PLAY_BADGE}}

Access to installation depends on Google Play and your account. If the listing is unavailable or restricted to testers, you may not be able to install it. This link does not confirm public availability. Contact MedVeda support if you need help.

{{icon:info}}

Requirements

  • An Android phone with Google Play
  • An internet connection to sign in and browse; downloaded quizzes work offline. Paid Notes and videos require internet.
  • A valid email address and mobile number to register
  • Allow notifications if you want reminders and updates

After installing

  1. Register and verify your email.
  2. Select your course and academic year.
  3. Start with free videos and MCQ tests. Paid Note purchase availability depends on the installed release and product readiness. Google Play Billing is limited to dedicated testing builds.
