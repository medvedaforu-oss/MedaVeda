For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") we act as the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described below. Contact: medvedaforu@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps some data locally: an encrypted sign-in session (with its encryption key in device secure storage), downloaded notes and quizzes, and an offline database (SQLite) with queued quiz scores awaiting sync, recently viewed chapters, recent searches, study streak, study-reminder and notification-preference settings. Sign-out/account deletion ends the app session and requests removal of account-owned downloaded files and download records. Other local study history, queued activity, settings and caches may remain; clear app data or uninstall to remove those copies. Downloaded PDFs are not encrypted by the app; session encryption does not encrypt downloads.

3. Why we use your data, and consent

We process your data to provide the Service you asked for (account, content, purchases), to run and secure it (fraud/abuse prevention, error monitoring), to communicate with you, and to comply with law. Where the DPDP Act requires consent, we rely on the consent you give when you register and use the app; you can withdraw consent by deleting your account (section 9), and you can switch off push notifications in the app or your phone settings at any time. We may also process data for "legitimate uses" the DPDP Act recognises, such as complying with legal obligations.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that process it for us:

  • Supabase – database, authentication, file storage and server functions. Our Supabase project is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the Asia-Pacific (Seoul, ap-northeast-2) region, so your data is stored and processed outside India.
  • Razorpay – payment processing. Razorpay receives the details needed to complete a payment and has its own privacy policy.
  • Expo (Expo Push Notification service) – delivers push notifications to your device using your push token.
  • Sentry – crash and error reporting when a release DSN is configured; performance tracing, screenshots and session replay are disabled.
  • Google Play / Google – app distribution, earlier purchase verification and refunds. This includes purchase identifiers and product IDs needed for those services. Google also provides Google Fonts on our website (see section 10).
  • Account-email provider – Supabase Auth uses its configured provider to deliver verification and password-reset emails. Resend is the intended provider; the live SMTP/email-hook configuration still requires verification. The provider receives your email address and message content. The contact form opens your own email app.
  • Authorities – where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account and study data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete the data described on our Account & Data Deletion page. Account data is kept while the account is active. After account deletion, personal data is erased within 30 days, except the fraud-prevention records described below and records we must keep by law (payment, invoice and tax records, kept for up to 8 years under Indian tax and accounting laws). The stated retention period for crash reports and usage events is up to 12 months, after which it is deleted or kept only in anonymised, aggregated form; enforcement in provider settings and cleanup jobs still requires verification. Backups are overwritten in the normal cycle.

Fraud-prevention email/phone hashes, the original account ID and first-purchase timestamp have a retention cutoff of 180 days from the first recorded purchase, including when an account is later deleted. Deleting an account does not restart that period. We have not yet confirmed automatic cleanup of these records; deletion at that cutoff is not guaranteed while cleanup is pending.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Supabase, row-level access rules so that students can read only their own account data, secure device storage for your session, time-limited signed links for paid notes, and restricted administrative access. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your password private. If a personal data breach affects you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you may: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold and who we share it with; ask us to correct or update inaccurate data (you can edit your name and profile in the app); ask us to erase your data; withdraw consent; nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity; and have your grievance addressed. To exercise these rights, use the in-app options or email medvedaforu@gmail.com from your registered email address. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

8. Children

MedVeda is intended for students of degree-level AYUSH courses and is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please use the app only with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. Contact us if a child has provided personal data so we can review and handle the request.

9. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account, then confirm Delete and wait for success. If deletion fails, retry or contact support. If you cannot sign in, email medvedaforu@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my MedVeda account" and your registered mobile number. Email requests are handled within 30 days of the request after identity checks. Deletion removes purchased access and cannot be undone. See the Account & Data Deletion page for retained exceptions and local-device cleanup.

10. Website, cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics scripts on it. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. The sign-up verification and password-reset pages read a secure token from the link you opened, use it only to talk to Supabase, and remove it from the address bar immediately; they do not store it in cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage.

11. International transfers

Your data is processed in the Republic of Korea (Supabase region) and in other countries where our providers operate. The DPDP Act permits transfers to countries other than those the Government of India restricts.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Effective date" above shows the latest version; for material changes we will notify you in the app or by email.
