## Failure and recovery decisions

Before COMMIT, stop and roll back on drift, failed checks, lock timeout or SQL errors. Independently confirm schema/function/ACL/ledger/data state before any approved retry. Do not mark a migration applied on failure.

After lost connection near COMMIT, treat outcome as UNKNOWN. An independent read-only session must compare the full ledger version/source arrays, function/ACL/owner/path identities, columns/indexes/triggers, queues and preservation predicates. Exact pre-state permits review of a retry; exact post-state requires postchecks; mixed/unknown state requires incident escalation. Never blindly rerun, pre-mark versions or repair the ledger to force progression.

After a known commit, prefer additive forward recovery with traffic held. Retain MV003/MV005/MV006 protections. Do not restore a verifier lacking authentication/commitment proof or a helper that acknowledges unknown reversals. Any bundle rollback requires separate schema/security review. Never roll back financial history or restore an older database over later provider facts as an automatic remedy. A full disaster restore requires the owner-approved recovery checklist and provider reconciliation before resume.
